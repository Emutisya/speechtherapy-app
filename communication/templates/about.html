CLEAR ABOUT WHAT THIS IS

Connection, with care.

Communication is more than speech.

Fluent is a caregiver-supported communication aid and optional practice space, designed with autistic children in mind. It does not diagnose, treat, assess pronunciation, or replace a speech-language professional or a personalized AAC system.

There is no requirement to speak, make eye contact, hide stimming, or complete an activity. No streaks, rankings, accuracy scores, or forced repetition.

Ask the child what is comfortable. Honor no, stop, all done, and requests for a break. Never withhold food, water, help, or access to communication.

Private by design, not a clinical vault.

The server accepts connections only from this device. No analytics, advertising, cloud AI, microphone recording, or third-party scripts are used. The core app works without an internet connection after installation.

There are no user passwords or separate access permissions. Local progress is plain SQLite data, not encrypted by this app. Use a protected device account and disk encryption. Other browser tabs and people using this device may see saved spaces.

Board messages and writing are not saved. Optional observation saving needs an explicit caregiver choice. A browser session cookie identifies the active space. Clearing cookies does not delete the database.

Honest about the data.

The original repository contains adult TED transcripts, phonetic vectors, semantic embeddings, and serialized research artifacts. These are not child speech recordings or validated autism therapy datasets.

None of those legacy datasets or serialized models is loaded by the child-facing app. Activities and cards are original, manually authored starter content, not clinically validated therapy.

The repository's dataset report documents measured structure, quality checks, missing licenses, and limits. No efficacy or diagnostic claims are supported by these data.

When to ask for support.

Work with a qualified speech-language professional for individualized goals, access needs, language, motor planning, hearing, or swallowing concerns. This English-only starter board will not fit every child.

If a child indicates pain or distress, pause and seek appropriate help. In an emergency, contact local emergency services; do not use this app as an emergency communication service.

Accessibility is a design goal, not a certification. Test the board with the child and their access method. A printed board is available from Communicate.
